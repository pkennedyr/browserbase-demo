    Granola is the AI notepad for people in back-to-back meetings, trusted by top founders, VCs, and recruiters. And now it's available on your iPhone. Stay present and capture every conversation on-the-go — perfect for coffee catch-ups, phone calls, conference days, or just distilling your thoughts. Just open the app, tap start or make a call, and Granola instantly begins transcribing. Granola creates a shareable summary with highlights, decisions, and action items. BEAUTIFUL NOTES, ZERO EFFORT • Accurately transcribes meetings and phone calls, even in noisy environments • Lets you stay focused on the conversation instead of taking notes • Gives you clarity on key moments, decisions, and action items ONE-TAP CAPTURE • Start and stop note-taking straight from your Lock Screen with a single tap • Take notes on outbound phone calls directly from the iPhone app • Create an ad-hoc note from within the app • Use the Lock Screen or Home Screen widgets for easy access CHAT WITH NOTES • Ask questions about your notes and meeting attendees • Extract new insights and refer back to important details • Get clarity on action items and help drafting follow-ups SHARE EFFORTLESSLY • Send polished summaries to attendees in seconds • Edit notes and formatting to fit your needs • View and manage your notes across both iPhone and Mac Here's how it works: • Start a new note from your Lock Screen, Home Page widget, or in the app • Sync with Google Calendar to launch notes for upcoming events • Stay present while Granola transcribes in the background • Afterward, share notes with attendees or keep them private • Chat with your notes to get answers and uncover new insights • Search, view, and manage all your notes in one place Try Granola for a few meetings or calls today. It's free to get started. Need support?     • Free version transcription block of unreadable text

      Sep 9
      Doritos Chief

      I wanted to try an AI to transcribe meetings for my nonprofit, since it is legally required, and would simplify note taking. The summary Granola AI created is great, but is no better than any other generic AI tool. The transcription seemed to work well too, but the free version is setup so that if you export, or copy/paste, you lose 100% of the formatting and end up with a massive unreadable block of text. You can use other AI tools to at least separate different speakers, but different dialogue from an individual speakers is still all mashed together in a massive paragraph (imagine if some one presents for awhile). As well, there is no functional, like a scroll bar, to allow users review the transcript in the app, so you can only see the last few lines of dialogue from the meeting. My guess this is all to incentivize people to buy upgrades, but if an app cannot perform the basics, why would I expect upgrades to be better. After looking around at tools, I am disappointed such a highly rated app performed so poorly, and I would recommend Granola rethink offering a free version with such limitations.

    • Phone issues

      10/22/2025
      Sal Hab

      I love the phone feature. Very clever. However, a couple of bugs: 1. It's laggy in the UI responsiveness. For example, when I toggle the speakerphone from earpiece on and off, it's got about a 1-second delay. 2. If I call somebody and they don't answer, it still tries to generate a note, which I don't think is useful. 3. Better organization of call logs - calls to the same person should be grouped, etc.4. Notification after the call telling me my notes are ready shows the phone number rather than the name it should be mapped to. 5. Call stack UI builds up, and you have to dismiss each individually. So, if I had three phone calls all in a row through the Granola app, when I come back to the Granola app, you have to dismiss all of them, even though they're not all notes. You just see a blank screen with the person that you called. Dismissing is also not easy. If you try to slide it down, you kind of have to hit the down arrow instead. 6. Lots more, but this is my first day using it…I will change my review back to five stars once you acknowledge you received this feedback. I just wanted to get your attention once you fix this.
